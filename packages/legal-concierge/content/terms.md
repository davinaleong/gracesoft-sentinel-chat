# Sentinel Concierge — Terms & Conditions

**Effective date:** 13 August 2026
**Version:** 1.0.0

By messaging Sentinel Concierge on WhatsApp or Telegram, you agree to these terms.

## 1. What Concierge does

Concierge is an AI assistant that answers questions about the business using its published FAQ knowledge base, and helps you check availability and book appointments. It is not a human team member — see our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for how we disclose this.

## 2. AI-generated answers

Answers to general questions are generated by an AI language model, grounded in the business's own knowledge base. While we've configured it not to guess or fabricate answers it isn't confident about, AI-generated text can still be imperfect. If something matters — pricing, availability, or anything time-sensitive — confirm it with a member of the team before relying on it.

## 3. Bookings

A booking is only confirmed once Concierge replies telling you so — a slot being offered to you is not a confirmed booking until you select it and receive that confirmation. The business may need to reschedule or cancel a booking in exceptional circumstances (e.g. an unforeseen closure); we'll aim to notify you as far in advance as possible if that happens.

## 4. Acceptable use

Please don't use Concierge to send abusive, unlawful, or fraudulent content, or attempt to manipulate it into behaving outside its intended purpose (for example, trying to get it to ignore its own guardrails). We reserve the right to stop responding to a conversation that does this.

## 5. No liability for AI output

Concierge's responses are provided "as is." To the extent permitted by law, GraceSoft is not liable for decisions made in reliance on an AI-generated response where that response turns out to be inaccurate, except where required by applicable consumer protection law.
