# Sentinel Cook — Terms & Conditions

**Effective date:** 13 August 2026
**Version:** 1.0.0

By messaging Sentinel Cook on WhatsApp or Telegram, you agree to these terms.

## 1. What Cook does

Cook is an AI assistant that identifies dishes from photos you send and generates a recipe — ingredients, method, substitutions, serving suggestions, and approximate nutritional information.

## 2. Nutritional information is an estimate

Nutritional values are AI-generated estimates, not laboratory measurements. **They are not intended for medical, dietary, or allergen-management decisions.** If you have a medical condition, allergy, or dietary requirement where accuracy matters, verify ingredients and nutrition independently — do not rely on Cook's output alone.

## 3. Recipe accuracy

Cook does its best to identify the dish in your photo and produce a sensible recipe, but it can misidentify a dish or produce an imperfect recipe, especially from an unclear or ambiguous photo. Use your own judgement when cooking.

## 4. Acceptable use

Please don't send photos or messages that are abusive, unlawful, or unrelated to food/recipes, or attempt to manipulate Cook into behaving outside its intended purpose. We reserve the right to stop responding to a conversation that does this.
