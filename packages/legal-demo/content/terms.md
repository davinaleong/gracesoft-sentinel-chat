# Sentinel Demo — Terms & Conditions

**Effective date:** 24 August 2026

## 1. What Sentinel Demo does

Sentinel Demo is a demonstration of GraceSoft's Sentinel product line, letting you try both Sentinel Concierge (FAQ answers, appointment booking) and Sentinel Cook (dish recognition, recipe generation) in a single conversation. You can switch which one you're talking to at any time — see the in-chat instructions for how. It is not a human team member for either service — see our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for how we disclose this.

## 2. It's a demo, not a live business channel

This is a demonstration environment. Appointments made through the Concierge half of the demo are demonstration bookings, not real commitments from a real business, and any data in this environment (conversation history, session state, demo bookings) may be reset or deleted at any time without notice. Don't rely on the demo for anything time-sensitive or real.

## 3. AI-generated answers

Answers to general questions are generated by an AI language model, grounded in the same knowledge base Sentinel Concierge uses in production. Recipe suggestions from Sentinel Cook are also AI-generated from a photo you send. While we've configured both not to guess or fabricate answers they aren't confident about, AI-generated text can still be imperfect — treat everything in the demo as illustrative, not authoritative.
