# Asset Mapping

## Wordmark

Suitable for navbars and documents.

- `wm.png/svg` - Combination of company and product names in brand colours.
- `wm-b.png/svg` - Combination of company and product names in black.
- `wm-w.png/svg` - Combination of company and product names in white.

## Logo

Suitable for site icons and app icons.

- `logo.png/svg` - Product initials in brand colours.
- `logo-b.png/svg` - Product initials in black.
- `logo-w.png/svg` - Product initials in white.
- `logo-square.png` - Product initials in brand colours on a square, transparent canvas. Use this as the site's icon.
